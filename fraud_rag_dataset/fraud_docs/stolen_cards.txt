Stolen card fraud patterns:

- Card numbers reported stolen or leaked online
- Unauthorized use at multiple merchants within a short period
- Transactions inconsistent with cardholder behavior
- Purchases at high-risk merchants or unusual countries
- Small transactions followed by high-value transactions (testing)
- Transactions immediately after reported card loss or theft

Industry Notes:
- Real-time card monitoring systems flag unusual patterns
- EMV chip transactions reduce counterfeit risk but online remains high
- Velocity and geography rules often detect stolen card activity
