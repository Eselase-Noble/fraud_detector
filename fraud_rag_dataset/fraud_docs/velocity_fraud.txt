Velocity fraud is characterized by:

- Rapid sequence of transactions on the same card or account
- Increasing transaction amounts over short time intervals
- Multiple merchants or categories in a short time window
- Multiple attempts with declined transactions
- Sudden changes in spending patterns

Industry Notes:
- Banks enforce throttling rules to prevent high-risk velocity transactions
- Combining velocity with location and device data increases detection accuracy
- Real-time scoring engines flag accounts exceeding normal transaction thresholds
